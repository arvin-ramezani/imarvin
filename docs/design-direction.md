# Design Direction

Status: Personal Studio (P01) and Signal Studio (P09) selected; detailed contracts/values draft. Updated: 2026-10-03.

## Product and expression

Enter Arvin's space → choose work → inspect decisions/evidence → explore again or contact. P05–P07 fix one featured entry, projects-first browsing, and summary then sections.

Signal Studio uses bright neutral space, dark ink, cobalt selection, bold readable work-led type, an offset identity anchor, and a main inspection stage with contextual return. Its signature is composition plus meaningful state change; it cannot be reduced to an accent button or large name.

Premium means crafted hierarchy and recognizable identity. Simple means few meaningful choices and predictable recovery. Company entries remain logos-only; optional project evidence lives inside inspection. Cancelled/offline/no-media work remains complete.

## Rejection conditions

Reject the fixed CV skeleton, chronological résumé rows, uniform card walls, generic metrics/sidebar dashboard, fabricated product evidence, hover-only controls, forced tours, and cosmetic creativity without useful inspection.

No visual quality, usability, accessibility conformance, or production readiness is certified by written documents. Exact typeface/rendered target, truthful public content/contact, and architecture remain open. No code, images, merge, or deployment is authorized.
