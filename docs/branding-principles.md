## Apply the knowledge

| Principle | Application to imarvin |
| --- | --- |
| Define the outcome before presentation | Job and freelance opportunities determine what work gets prominence |
| Reach a specific relevant audience | Explain responsibility for employers and solved problems for clients |
| Support claims with real work | Link contributions, decisions, and evidence to each story |
| Start from demonstrated experience | Web applications and ecommerce are initial evidence areas; expertise claims require support |
| Be honest about learning | Describe architecture/UX interests as development areas when not proven |
| Use consistent ideas and behavior | Repeat problem, contribution, decision, outcome across work stories |
| Show personality through perspective | Explain why a decision mattered and what Arvin learned |
| Build portable trust | Shareable experience pages and contact paths support relationships beyond a social profile |

## Working positioning

Proposed association: a curious full-stack builder whose work, decisions, and experiments reveal his personality. Concrete professional responsibility remains visible within stories.

A learner in one area can be experienced in another. Do not label Arvin an expert in every technology or reduce existing professional work to beginner status.

## Writing rules

- Lead with the concrete problem and individual contribution.
- Use specific verbs and familiar language.
- State constraints, collaboration, and unfinished work honestly.
- Explain outcomes only when supported; absence of a metric is acceptable.
- Mention technologies where they explain work, not as the main story.
- Treat AI assistance transparently when material to the workflow; do not infer AI authorship from the repository.

Do not manufacture a contrarian belief, personal mission, customer quote, performance claim, or availability promise. Ask for the underlying experience first.

## Evidence limits

A code repository can establish that features or tests exist. It cannot alone prove who implemented them, customer impact, production readiness, or the owner's exact employment responsibilities. Confirm those before publishing.
